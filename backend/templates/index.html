About FloodIntel

FloodIntel is an intelligent flash flood prediction and early warning system designed to monitor flood-prone regions across North and North-Eastern India. It combines rainfall, live weather information, river gauge observations, satellite imagery and other environmental data to identify potential flood risks at an early stage.

By analysing multiple data sources and continuously monitoring changing conditions, FloodIntel helps identify emerging flood risks and supports faster, data-driven early warning and response.
